<Warning>
VMs are in beta and are more limited than [Docker-based instances](/guides/instances/docker-environment). Choose a Docker-based instance unless your workload needs a VM.

- **Mostly consumer GPUs:** most machines that support VMs have consumer GPUs.
- **Slow to start:** VMs with large amounts of RAM can take 30 minutes or more to start.
- **No Cloud Sync:** data moves only by copying a whole VM to another VM.
- **More failed launches:** VMs fail to launch more often than Docker-based instances.
</Warning>
