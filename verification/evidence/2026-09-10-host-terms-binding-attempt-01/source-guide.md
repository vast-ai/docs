# What the Terms can establish

Source: [Vast Terms of Service](https://vast.ai/terms), published version
10 November 2025, retrieved on 10 September 2026. The independent rendered
capture is [terms-source-01.json](terms-source-01.json). Its SHA-256 is
`936cc633ff36966ade3b063ee8eaf6bdd61cb9fd9d2f79201b03c9d5fbb84c38`.

The source page has no section IDs for these headings. Citations therefore
link to the official page and name the section and item number; the reviewer
also opens the exact retained excerpt beside the statement being reviewed.

| Statement in Workload Policy / Restricted Activity | Applicable source | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Terms list prohibited activity | Prohibited Activities, introduction | A published rule, not observed enforcement. |
| Illegal activity and knowingly sharing unlawful or abusive content | Prohibited Activities, items 3, 13, 14 and 21 | Keep the content actions and the “knowingly” condition. This does not establish a host monitoring or escalation duty. |
| Malware, spam and interference with Website/network services | Prohibited Activities, items 5, 12 and 20; Hosting Agreement, License and Responsibilities | Keep the scope of each rule. The Provider-specific Agreement clause and user-facing Terms complement one another; neither proves a general escalation workflow. |
| Intellectual-property infringement and renter-data privacy | Prohibited Activities, item 15; Hosting Agreement, Operation and Maintenance | The Terms establish the IP restriction. The Agreement separately establishes the Provider's data-handling restriction. Neither establishes an instruction to escalate every concern. |
| Export restrictions | Export Compliance | Keep the user, destination/person/end-use and required-authorization limits. Vast's right to suspend is not an instruction for hosts to suspend rentals. |
| Mining after buying service credits with a credit card | Prohibited Activities, item 22 | The credit-card condition is essential. This does not establish a blanket mining ban or permission for every other payment method. |

These citations can resolve a missing-source finding for the matching rule.
They cannot establish technical enforcement, runtime results, account-specific
acceptance, or an unstated operational procedure. Documentation pages, the
review model and the graph are not proof of their own statements.

The final result records which corrected occurrences actually passed the
source-binding and reviewer checks. This guide is interpretation/navigation
context, not a replacement for the captured official source.
