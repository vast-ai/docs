  • Price option names, units, hypothetical examples, pricing advice and navigation labels do not set commercial terms. Existing-rental price persistence, fees, payout minimum/timing, accepted rate and actual revenue accrual remain authoritative financial/contract claims.
  • Payout/invoice screen labels, export actions, team context and invoice fields are technical/UI. Paid and Pending assertions need backend event mapping, not just a screenshot. A $20 minimum, Friday schedule or disclaimed provider liability remains a financial rule.
  • Bare technical contract IDs/events are not legal terms; complete lead-ins such as While a rental contract is active must still be applied when their list items state duties. Do not erase true obligations while removing heading leakage.
  • Support/dedicated-account claims may mix best practice with an assertion that a setup is unsupported. Preserve and separately source the asserted support boundary; do not call the whole mixed paragraph editorial.
  • The full Terms/applicable agreement source must support exact mining, export, private-data and noninterference language. An agreement ban on reading data does not automatically prove a ban on stopping containers or copying logs; retain unsatisfied subsets.
  • Source-first is not owner-free in every future case. Owner escalation remains correct for an actual unavailable governing source, contradiction, new policy choice, or unresolved applicability judgment, but this audit did not establish any owner unavailable merely because evidence is missing.
