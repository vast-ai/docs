How do I get a paper copy of my tax form?

If you're eligible to receive a tax form (e.g., Form 1099-K, 1099-INT), we'll post the form to your account and email it to you. If you also need an additional paper copy, please contact us.

Note: We won't issue a Form 1099-K if you don't meet the reporting thresholds.

Here’s how to locate your tax documents:

  • Personal accounts go to the PayPal Statements & Tax Center on your desktop or from within the app.
  • Business accounts go to the Tax Statement page.
