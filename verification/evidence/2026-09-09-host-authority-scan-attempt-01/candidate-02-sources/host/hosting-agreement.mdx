## What you commit to, in plain language

The [Hosting Agreement — Hardware as a Service and Performance of Services](https://cloud.vast.ai/host/agreement) sets out provider duties for hardware and services. Use this operational checklist when managing active rentals; named agreement clauses below support the duties they describe:

- Provide the advertised hardware and services. [Hosting Agreement — Performance of Services](https://cloud.vast.ai/host/agreement) assigns responsibility for properly running, troubleshooting, and maintaining the hardware.
- Dedicate rented hardware to renter workloads and avoid interfering with active jobs. See the [Vast CLI guidance for rented machines](https://github.com/vast-ai/vast-cli/blob/ecf32efa1d8d2f110f7de4118c30698bb7ae2fbd/vastai/cli/commands/machines.py#L247-L251).
- Keep client data isolated and protected. [Hosting Agreement — Operation and Maintenance](https://cloud.vast.ai/host/agreement) prohibits reviewing or retaining renter data, and [Hosting Agreement — Intellectual Property and Data Security](https://cloud.vast.ai/host/agreement) requires reasonable safeguards.
- Use reasonable safeguards and physical security around the hardware to protect Vast.ai Content. See [Hosting Agreement — Intellectual Property and Data Security](https://cloud.vast.ai/host/agreement).
- Provide the advertised services until each rental contract's rental end date.

For the offer and rental lifecycle, see [Hosting Overview: the rental contract](/host/hosting-overview#the-rental-contract).

## Data isolation

Do not read, download, store, review, print, or save renter data. See [Hosting Agreement — Operation and Maintenance](https://cloud.vast.ai/host/agreement).

Avoid disrupting renter containers during host troubleshooting, and do not review renter data. [Hosting Agreement — License and Responsibilities](https://cloud.vast.ai/host/agreement) prohibits interference with the software or its data, while [Hosting Agreement — Operation and Maintenance](https://cloud.vast.ai/host/agreement) prohibits reviewing or retaining renter data. For the host-side investigation checklist, see [Workload Policy](/host/workload-policy).
