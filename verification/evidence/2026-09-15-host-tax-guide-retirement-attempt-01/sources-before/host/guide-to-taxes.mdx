The [Hosting Agreement — Independent Contractors](https://cloud.vast.ai/host/agreement) treats Vast and the provider as independent contractors. Its Hardware as a Service Payments section assigns the provider responsibility for taxes connected with its activities. Keep earnings records and seek tax advice for your filings.

**Withholding:** Review your payout records and ask Vast Support and your payout provider to confirm any withholding that applies to your account.

## International Hosts

The [Hosting Agreement — Hardware as a Service Payments](https://cloud.vast.ai/host/agreement) assigns the provider responsibility for taxes connected with its activities. Seek advice on the filing requirements that apply where you operate.

## United States Hosts

Depending on the provider, your eligibility, and the platform's tax-form setup, a tax form may be available. See [PayPal tax-form delivery guidance](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/how-do-i-get-a-paper-copy-of-my-tax-form-help672) and [Stripe tax-form delivery guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/express-dashboard-taxes). Before filing:

- Keep your tax information current with your payment provider. Follow your account’s instructions; [Stripe’s tax-form guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/express-dashboard-taxes) explains its update process and availability limits.
- Check your provider’s current tax-form eligibility rules for the relevant tax year and location. See [PayPal’s US tax-form guidance](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/how-do-i-get-a-paper-copy-of-my-tax-form-help672) and [IRS Form 1099-K guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/understanding-your-form-1099-k).
- Check which income to report and when to file and pay. Start with the [IRS gig-economy tax guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/gig-economy-tax-center). If California tax rules apply to you, also review the [California FTB guidance](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/industries/gig-economy.html).

Contact your payment provider directly with any questions about your tax forms.

## By Payout Method

**Stripe Connect** Stripe Express can deliver tax forms and let eligible connected accounts review their tax information, depending on the platform's configuration and delivery settings. Follow the tax-form instructions provided for your account; [Stripe tax-form delivery guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/express-dashboard-taxes) explains these conditions and directs Express users to [Stripe Express support](https://support.stripe.com/express).

**PayPal** PayPal's [US tax-form delivery guidance](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/how-do-i-get-a-paper-copy-of-my-tax-form-help672) says eligible forms are posted to the account and emailed, and explains where personal and business accounts find their tax documents. Use PayPal's current reporting-threshold guidance to check eligibility, and contact PayPal support with questions.

**Wise** If you are a **US-based host** receiving payouts via Wise, ask Vast Support whether Vast needs tax information for your account. If it does, confirm which form is required and the approved secure submission method before sending it.

If you are an international host receiving payouts via Wise, please refer to the International Hosts section above.

## Common Questions

<span id="does-vastai-handle-vat" aria-hidden="true" />
### Does Vast.ai handle VAT?

Ask Vast Billing or Support to confirm the VAT treatment for your payouts or charges. Seek tax advice about the obligations that apply to your own business.

<span id="is-vat-shown-on-invoices" aria-hidden="true" />
### Is VAT shown on invoices?

Download the relevant invoice from **Earnings > Payout History** and check it for tax information. If VAT information is absent or unclear, ask Vast Billing or Support about that invoice.
