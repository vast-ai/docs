Manage payouts from **Earnings** in the console. For tax forms and withholding, see the [Tax Guide for Hosts](/host/guide-to-taxes).

For team-owned host machines, earnings and payout settings belong to the team account. See [Host Teams](/host/host-teams#earnings-and-payouts).

## Earnings Page

![Vast.ai Earnings page with balance summaries and an earnings chart](/images/console-earning.webp)

The Earnings page shows:

- **Current Balance**: earned but not yet paid.
- **Total Earnings**: lifetime host, referral, and template earnings.
- **Total Rental Earnings**: lifetime machine-rental earnings.
- **Total Referral Earnings**: lifetime referral earnings.
- **Payout History**: generated payout records and invoices.

## Payout Methods

The payout providers shown under [Earnings > Payout Account](https://cloud.vast.ai/earnings/) are:

- Wise
- PayPal
- Stripe

Before selecting a method, confirm that the provider works in your region, your account is verified, and it can receive business payments.

## Payout Account

Use **Earnings > Payout Account** to connect, view, remove, or switch payout providers.

Only one payout account can be active at a time. To switch accounts, first deactivate the current account, then activate the new one.

## Payout Schedule

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Invoice minimum | At least $20 USD per [published payout guidance](https://docs.vast.ai/host/payment#minimum-payout-threshold) |
| Invoice generation | Weekly on Fridays per [published payout guidance](https://docs.vast.ai/host/payment#invoice-generation) |
| Payment timing | [Published payout guidance](https://docs.vast.ai/host/payment#payment-timeline) says a first payout typically takes up to two weeks. Provider processing may take longer depending on the provider and your region. Separately, [Hosting Agreement — Hardware as a Service Payments](https://cloud.vast.ai/host/agreement) specifies weekly billing periods and payment payable within 14 days of a completed billing period. |
| Paid status | Vast submitted the payout; the provider may still be processing it |

Balances below $20 roll forward until the minimum threshold is reached, per [published payout guidance](https://docs.vast.ai/host/payment#minimum-payout-threshold).

## Payout History And Invoices

Start from **Account > Earnings**.

Use the **Earnings Chart** controls to download CSV or PDF earnings data when available.

Use **Payout History** to:

- Filter payout records by date.
- Download CSV or PDF payout records and invoices.
- Review invoice status.

Use **Payout Account** to manage connected payout providers. It is not the invoice export area.

For billing-history invoices with itemized usage charges, open **Billing**, use **Generate Billing History**, and check **Include Charges**.

To customize invoice details, update **Settings > Invoice Information**, or open [Settings](https://cloud.vast.ai/settings/). For team-owned host machines, switch to the team context first so generated invoices use the team or business details.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<span id="when-paid" aria-hidden="true" />
### When will I get paid?

[Published payout guidance](https://docs.vast.ai/host/payment#payment-timeline) says a first payout typically takes up to two weeks. Provider processing may take longer depending on the provider and your region. The contractual clock is different: [Hosting Agreement — Hardware as a Service Payments](https://cloud.vast.ai/host/agreement) specifies weekly billing periods, with payment payable within 14 days of a completed billing period. Check the invoice and provider status for the actual payout.

<span id="paid-invoice" aria-hidden="true" />
### Why does my invoice show Paid when funds have not arrived?

Paid means Vast submitted the payout to your selected provider. It does not guarantee that Wise, PayPal, or Stripe has completed settlement.

Check that your payout account is active, verified, allowed to receive business payments, and not under review or hold.

<span id="pending-invoice" aria-hidden="true" />
### What does Pending mean?

The invoice was generated but has not entered the payment phase. It changes to Paid after Vast submits the payout.

<span id="direct-bank-transfer" aria-hidden="true" /><span id="how-do-i-set-up-payouts" aria-hidden="true" />
### Can Vast send direct bank transfers?

[Vast's published payout guidance](https://docs.vast.ai/host/payment#can-vast-ai-send-my-payout-via-direct-bank-transfer-ach-swift-wire-transfer-etc-) says direct bank transfers, including ACH, wire and SWIFT, are unavailable.

<span id="invoice-not-generating" aria-hidden="true" />
### Why are invoices not generating?

[Published payout guidance](https://docs.vast.ai/host/payment#invoice-generation) says invoices require a valid payout method connected and a balance of at least $20 USD.

<span id="manual-invoice" aria-hidden="true" />
### Can I generate an invoice manually?

You can download payout records from **Account > Earnings > Payout History**. For billing history, use **Billing > Generate Billing History** with **Include Charges** enabled.

<span id="vast-user-earnings" aria-hidden="true" />
### How can I have earnings as a Vast user?

Hosts earn from machine rentals. Users may also earn referral or template income; see the [referral program](/guides/reference/referral-program).
