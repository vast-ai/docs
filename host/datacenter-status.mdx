Datacenter status covers equipment in professionally managed facilities that meets Vast's partner requirements. [Vast Compliance — Secure Cloud](https://vast.ai/compliance) describes Secure Cloud eligibility; Vast's [January 2024 datacenter article](https://vast.ai/article/Navigating-Data-Center-Compliance) describes a blue datacenter label. Confirm the applicable certification requirements below before applying.

## Benefits

- Vast's [January 2024 datacenter article](https://vast.ai/article/Navigating-Data-Center-Compliance) describes a blue datacenter label.
- Secure Cloud search eligibility, described under Secure Cloud (Trusted Datacenters) on the [Compliance page](https://vast.ai/compliance).
- The [Certified Data Center program](https://vast.ai/data-center-application), under Priority Placement, describes higher search placement for certified machines.
- The [Certified Data Center program](https://vast.ai/data-center-application), under Dedicated Support, describes direct access to Vast’s team for onboarding, troubleshooting, and operational support.

## Requirements

- Confirm the applicable certification requirement with Vast's datacenter Product/Compliance team: the current [Compliance page — Secure Cloud](https://vast.ai/compliance) says ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are encouraged but not strictly required, while the [October 2024 security article — Third-Party Certifications](https://vast.ai/article/security-and-compliance-at-vast-ai) describes ISO 27001 as a minimum. These published sources differ.
- Equipment must be owned by a registered business. See [Certified Data Center program, Verified Business](https://vast.ai/data-center-application).
- For Secure Cloud datacenter partners, the [Compliance page](https://vast.ai/compliance), under Auditing & oversight, describes audits of ownership structure, identity, and source of funds. The [Certified Data Center program](https://vast.ai/data-center-application), under Verified Business, separately requires equipment owned by a registered business and owner identity verification.
- A signed expanded hosting agreement with Data Processing Agreement coverage, as described under Legal & Contractual Protections on the [Compliance page](https://vast.ai/compliance). Obtain the current datacenter agreement during the application process.
- Owner identity verification. See Auditing & oversight on the [Compliance page](https://vast.ai/compliance).
- At least 5 GPU servers with flagship-class hardware, as listed on [Vast Compliance — Secure Cloud](https://vast.ai/compliance). If the servers are not yet listed, confirm with the datacenter team what evidence of equipment they accept during application.

## Apply

Prepare:

- Government-issued ID for business owner(s).
- Business registration or certificate of good standing.
- Datacenter name, address, and certificates.
- Contract or invoice linking the business to the datacenter.
- Any additional due-diligence documents Vast requests.

Apply at [vast.ai/data-center-application](https://vast.ai/data-center-application).
